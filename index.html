What is the programme

You help students find opportunities they'd never have heard of otherwise.

As a Global Student Ambassador, you help promote elite student events and competitions — including Harvard Crimson Global Essay Competition, Harvard Crimson Case Competition, the LSE Generate Global Innovation Challenge, and the High School Academic Research Competition (SARC) — to your school, your friends, and your wider community.

It's informal, flexible, and built around the time you already have. No quotas, no scripts — just genuine recommendations backed by materials we provide.

Your role, in practice

Set aside 1–2 hours a week to discuss, share, and promote our competitions with your peers. We give you the materials and strategy — every sign-up made with your personal code builds credit toward consulting advice and vouchers.

What you get

The more sign-ups you bring in, the more you benefit.

01

Crimson credit & credit vouchers

Earn $5 USD in credit for every registration you bring in. Once you've built up $100 (20 registrations), cash it out as a credit voucher — or exchange it for an hour of 1:1 mentoring on university applications, essays, extracurriculars, and interview prep.

Sign-ups carry over from one competition to the next, so nothing you earn is wasted.
02

Webinars and workshops

Free access to webinars covering US/UK admissions, essay writing, and debate and public speaking skills. Got a topic you want covered? Suggest it and we'll look into arranging it.

03

Internship opportunities

Top Ambassadors get the chance to intern directly with our competitions and projects — including Yale Model United Nations in Dubai, Singapore, and Latin America.

04

Ambassador certificate

Bring in at least 3 sign-ups for a competition and receive an official Ambassador certificate — a credible addition to your college and scholarship applications.

05

A global network

Get access to an online community of like-minded, highly accomplished peers from around the world.
