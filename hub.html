Starter Kit

New here? Start with this.

New to the programme? Read this first — it covers everything you need to know. Refer back to the FAQ at the bottom for anything else.

As a Global Student Ambassador, you help promote elite student competitions — including the Harvard Crimson Global Essay Competition, Harvard Crimson Case Competition, and the LSE Generate Global Innovation Challenge — to your school, your friends, and your wider community.

You set aside 1–2 hours a week to share and promote our competitions with your peers. Every sign-up made with your personal code builds credit toward credit vouchers and mentoring.

You earn $5 USD in credit for every registration you bring in. Once you've built up $100 (20 registrations), you can cash it out as a credit voucher — or exchange it for an hour of 1:1 mentoring on university applications, essays, extracurriculars, and interview prep.

Sign-ups carry over from one competition to the next. There are also weekly bonus credits and round-end prizes for the top ambassadors.

You can add "Global Student Ambassador – powered by Crimson Education" as a position on LinkedIn under Volunteer Experience or Experience. Include your start date and describe it as "promoting elite international student competitions to peers globally, developing leadership and outreach skills."

Current Competition

What you're promoting right now.

LSE Generate Global Innovation Challenge 2026 — formerly known as Pitch for the Future (PFF), now with a powerful new collaborator: LSE Generate.

What is the LGIC?

Website: https://www.globinnovation.org/ — save it, pin it, print it — you'll need it!
Registration link: https://www.globinnovation.org/registernow

Formerly known as Pitch for the Future, the LSE Generate Global Innovation Challenge invites high school students worldwide to develop solutions to real-world problems through the lens of the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Hosted online in partnership with LSE Generate and Crimson Education, the competition builds creativity, problem-solving, and entrepreneurial confidence – connecting students with a global network of like-minded peers.
